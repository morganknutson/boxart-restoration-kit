# Game Boy / Color / Advance notes

Lessons from restoring ~2,500 Game Boy, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance boxes, spines and Game Pak labels.
Give these to your agents alongside `agents/RESTORER.md` (e.g. reference this file from the project's ANNOUNCEMENTS.md).

## Editions
- Product codes tell the edition: `DMG-xx-USA`, `CGB-xxxE`, `AGB-xxxE` (E = North America). European/UK codes end in
  e.g. `EUR`, `UKV`, `NOE` (Germany), `FAH` (France), `ESP`, `ITA`; Japanese boxes say `JPN` or have no Latin code.
- **Bilingual English/French North American boxes are the US edition.** EA, Activision, THQ, Ubisoft and others used one
  box for the US and Canada. Restore them as printed, French included. Agents will otherwise burn hours searching for an
  "English-only" US box that doesn't exist.
- Barcode company prefixes help: e.g. Nintendo 045496, THQ 785138, Activision 047875, Konami 083717, Majesco 096427,
  Ubisoft 008888, Acclaim 042244, Midway 031719, EA 014633. A prefix that doesn't fit the publisher, a missing product
  code, or a suspiciously clean, sparse layout often means a fan-made mock-up back — find a real retail photo or hold.
  One mock-up UPC seen on many fan backs: 6 25904 36390 9.

## Print details
- The Nintendo Seal of Quality is gold, lettering included — don't "correct" it to grey from faded scans.
- Body copy is usually regular weight, bullet lists often italic. Bold where the box is regular is wrong.
- Keep the box's own typos and odd spacing; don't add accents, punctuation, "Co.", ™ or © that aren't printed.
- Game Boy screenshots are 160×144 (Color the same), Advance 240×160: rebuild them on that pixel grid.
- Spine titles: reuse the restored front's title lettering (scaled/rotated); generated spine titles often scramble.

## Game Pak labels
- An extracted label is often small and soft (cut out of a cart render). Use real cart photos as the authority for print
  and colour; output a clean, flat sticker at ≥1200 px wide with square corners, no plastic, shadow or background.
- If the extraction wrongly includes cart plastic, output the sticker alone at the real sticker's aspect ratio.
- Reference photos from collector sites often carry a watermark: never reproduce it.
